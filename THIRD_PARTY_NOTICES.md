## Feather Icons

Several of the icons in `web/src/components/Icon.svelte` are copied or adapted from
[Feather](https://github.com/feathericons/feather) by Cole Bemis (version 4.29.2), redrawn as single compact SVG paths
on the same 24×24 grid:

- copied, with coordinates rounded: `settings` (Feather's `settings`), `moon` (`moon`), `alert` (`alert-triangle`),
  and the arrowhead of `reply` and `undo` (`corner-up-left`);
- adapted from the Feather design, redrawn: `command` (`command`), `sun` (`sun`), `external` (`external-link`), `mic`
  (`mic`), `list` (`list`), `refresh` (`refresh-cw`), `inbox` (`inbox`) and `trash` (`trash-2`).

The remaining icons are simple geometric shapes drawn for Nudge.
